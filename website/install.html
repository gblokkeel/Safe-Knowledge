Install as app on iPhone & iPad

📱 Install on iPhone or iPad (Safari)

1

🌐 Open in Safari

Make sure you're using Safari — Chrome and Firefox don't support "Add to Home Screen" on iOS.

2

⬆️ Tap the Share button

Tap the Share icon at the bottom of Safari (the box with an arrow pointing up).

3

➕ Add to Home Screen

Scroll down in the share sheet and tap "Add to Home Screen". Then tap "Add" in the top right.

4

🎉 Launch from your home screen

The SAFe KB icon will appear on your home screen. Tap it to launch the app in full-screen, just like a native app.

✅ Works offline! All 44 articles are cached automatically after the first launch. You can read any article on the train, on a plane, or without Wi-Fi.

🖥️ Hosting options

A

Local network (home/office)

Run python3 serve.py on your Mac/PC, then open http://<your-ip>:8080 in Safari on your iPhone/iPad (must be on the same Wi-Fi).

B

GitHub Pages (free, public URL)

Push the repo to GitHub and enable GitHub Pages on the main branch. Your app will be live at https://username.github.io/Safe-Knowledge-/website/.

C

Netlify / Vercel (free, custom domain)

Drag the website/ folder to netlify.com/drop for an instant public URL with HTTPS — required for PWA service workers.
