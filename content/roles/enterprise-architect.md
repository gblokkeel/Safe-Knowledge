# Enterprise Architect

## Overview

The **Enterprise Architect** promotes adaptive design and engineering practices and drives architectural initiatives for the portfolio. They work across multiple ARTs and value streams to ensure the technical and architectural direction of the portfolio supports strategic objectives.

Unlike the System Architect (who works within a single ART), the Enterprise Architect has portfolio-wide scope, helping to ensure that the technical decisions made by individual ARTs work together coherently at the enterprise level.

## Responsibilities

### Portfolio Technical Strategy
- Define and communicate the **Enterprise Architecture** vision
- Identify and prioritize **Enabler Epics** in the Portfolio Backlog
- Ensure architectural decisions support the portfolio's Strategic Themes
- Guide technology selection and platform decisions

### Cross-ART Coordination
- Ensure architectural coherence across multiple ARTs
- Identify and manage cross-ART technical dependencies
- Define architectural guidelines that apply across ARTs
- Facilitate cross-ART Communities of Practice

### Support LPM and Epic Analysis
- Support the Lean Business Case analysis for technical Epics
- Serve as Epic Owner for major Enabler Epics
- Participate in Portfolio Kanban analysis activities
- Assess technical feasibility of Business Epics

### Guide Technical Practices
- Establish and communicate enterprise development standards
- Promote Built-in Quality practices across the portfolio
- Guide the adoption of DevOps and Continuous Delivery
- Identify and mitigate technical risks at the portfolio level

### Participate in PI Planning
- Attend PI Planning across multiple ARTs (or key ARTs)
- Present architectural initiatives and cross-ART constraints
- Help resolve cross-ART technical dependencies

## Enterprise Architect vs. System Architect

| Aspect | System Architect | Enterprise Architect |
|--------|-----------------|---------------------|
| **Scope** | Single ART | Portfolio / Multiple ARTs |
| **Focus** | ART system integrity | Enterprise architecture coherence |
| **Events** | PI Planning, System Demo | Portfolio events, multiple PI Plannings |
| **Backlog impact** | Program Backlog Enablers | Portfolio Backlog Enabler Epics |
| **Technical depth** | Deep within the ART | Broad across the portfolio |

## Enterprise Architecture Principles

SAFe advocates for **Lean Architecture** — applying Lean and Agile principles to architecture:
- **Just Enough** — Avoid over-engineering; design for today's needs + anticipated near-future
- **Last Responsible Moment** — Defer irreversible architectural decisions
- **Set-Based Design** — Explore multiple options before committing
- **Emergent Architecture** — Architecture evolves; it is not defined upfront and locked

## Communities of Practice

Enterprise Architects often lead or co-sponsor enterprise-level Communities of Practice:
- **Enterprise Architecture CoP** — Cross-ART architecture discussions
- **Security CoP** — Security practices across the portfolio
- **Data Architecture CoP** — Data management standards and practices
- **Platform Engineering CoP** — Shared platform and infrastructure guidance
