# Scrum Master / Team Coach

## Overview

The **Scrum Master** (also called **Team Coach** in SAFe) is a servant leader and coach for the Agile Team. Their primary responsibilities are to facilitate team ceremonies, coach the team on Agile and SAFe practices, remove impediments, and foster continuous improvement.

The Scrum Master is NOT a project manager, NOT a team lead, and NOT a traditional manager. They serve the team by creating the conditions for the team to do their best work.

## Responsibilities

### Facilitate Team Ceremonies
- **Sprint Planning** — Facilitate scope negotiation and plan creation
- **Daily Stand-up** — Facilitate the 15-minute daily sync
- **Sprint Review** — Facilitate the demonstration and feedback session
- **Sprint Retrospective** — Facilitate the improvement discussion
- **Backlog Refinement** — Support the PO in facilitating story preparation

### Coach the Team
- Coach on Scrum, Kanban, and SAFe practices
- Help the team implement Built-in Quality practices (TDD, refactoring, CI)
- Support the team in reaching high performance
- Coach on Agile values and mindset — not just mechanics

### Remove Impediments
- Identify and remove blockers the team cannot resolve themselves
- Escalate impediments to the RTE when needed
- Track impediments to closure
- Protect the team from external disruptions

### Foster Continuous Improvement
- Lead meaningful Sprint Retrospectives
- Track and follow up on improvement actions
- Celebrate improvements and wins
- Support the team's journey to high performance

### Support the RTE
- Represent the team in ART-level ceremonies (Scrum of Scrums)
- Communicate team status and blockers to the RTE
- Participate in PO Sync when invited

### Support the Product Owner
- Help the PO maintain a healthy, prepared backlog
- Coach the PO on story writing and acceptance criteria
- Protect the PO from excessive stakeholder demands

## Scrum Master vs. Project Manager

| Aspect | Scrum Master | Project Manager |
|--------|-------------|----------------|
| **Authority** | None — serves the team | Formal authority over resources |
| **Focus** | Team health and process | Schedule, scope, cost |
| **Approach** | Servant leadership | Command and control |
| **Decision-making** | Enables team decisions | Makes decisions for the team |
| **Success metric** | Team performance improvement | On-time, on-budget delivery |
| **Impediments** | Removes them | Manages around them |

## The Scrum Master as Servant Leader

Servant leadership means:
1. **Listening first** — Understand before responding
2. **Empathy** — See situations from the team's perspective
3. **Healing** — Help individuals and teams grow from challenges
4. **Awareness** — Deep understanding of team dynamics
5. **Persuasion** — Influence through logic and collaboration, not authority
6. **Conceptualization** — Think beyond the immediate to the long-term
7. **Foresight** — Anticipate problems before they become crises
8. **Stewardship** — Protect and develop the team
9. **Commitment to growth** — Develop each person's potential
10. **Building community** — Foster belonging and cohesion

## The Three Roles the Scrum Master Serves

### 1. Servant to the Agile Team
- Ensure the team has what it needs to deliver
- Protect the team from interruption
- Facilitate, but don't direct

### 2. Servant to the Product Owner
- Help maintain a healthy, prepared backlog
- Support effective communication between PO and team
- Coach PO on story writing and priorities

### 3. Servant to the Organization
- Promote Agile values and practices beyond the team
- Be an agent of change
- Help the organization understand how to support Agile teams

## Scrum Master Metrics

| Metric | What It Indicates |
|--------|------------------|
| **Sprint Goal Achievement** | Team's ability to commit and deliver |
| **Sprint Velocity Trend** | Team performance over time |
| **Impediment Resolution Time** | How quickly blockers are cleared |
| **Retrospective Action Item Completion** | Continuous improvement effectiveness |
| **Team Health** | Engagement, psychological safety, collaboration |

## Scaling: SM in ART Events

In SAFe, the Scrum Master also participates at the ART level:

| ART Event | SM Role |
|-----------|---------|
| **PI Planning** | Facilitates team breakouts; helps team create plan and identify risks |
| **Scrum of Scrums** | Represents the team's impediments and dependencies |
| **PO Sync** | May attend to represent team dependencies |
| **Inspect & Adapt** | Participates as a team member; leads team's improvement contributions |
| **System Demo** | Supports team preparation and presentation |

## Common Challenges

- **Scrum Master as secretary** — Reduced to managing Jira boards; loses coaching role
- **Scrum Master as project manager** — Tracked tasks, assignments; undermines self-organization
- **Over-facilitation** — Solving problems for the team instead of helping the team solve them
- **Part-time Scrum Master** — Hard to build deep team relationships when split across teams or roles
- **Too much team, not enough organization** — Focusing only on the team without addressing systemic issues

## Part-Time Scrum Master Guidance

- **One SM per team** is strongly recommended
- Serving two teams is possible but challenging
- The SM should not simultaneously be the PO (creates conflict of interest)
- The SM should not simultaneously be a developer on the same team (limits objectivity)
