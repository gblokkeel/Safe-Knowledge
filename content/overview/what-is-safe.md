# What is SAFe?

## Overview

The **Scaled Agile Framework® (SAFe®)** is an online knowledge base of proven, integrated principles and practices for Lean-Agile development. It provides comprehensive guidance for work at the Enterprise, Portfolio, Value Stream, Program, and Team levels of an organization.

SAFe helps organizations scale Agile practices from individual teams to the entire enterprise, enabling them to deliver value faster, with higher quality and greater predictability, while improving employee engagement.

## Why SAFe?

Most enterprises face significant challenges when scaling Agile beyond individual teams:

- **Coordination problems** across multiple teams working on the same product
- **Alignment gaps** between business strategy and team execution
- **Inconsistent practices** that prevent true organizational agility
- **Slow time to market** despite team-level agility
- **Quality issues** that emerge at integration points

SAFe addresses these challenges by providing a structured approach to scaling Agile that preserves what works at the team level while adding the coordination mechanisms needed at scale.

## Key Benefits

Organizations adopting SAFe typically report:

| Benefit | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Faster Time to Market** | 30-75% faster delivery of value to customers |
| **Higher Quality** | Significant reduction in defects and rework |
| **Improved Productivity** | 20-50% increase in productivity |
| **Greater Employee Engagement** | Improved morale and reduced attrition |
| **Better Alignment** | Strategy-to-execution alignment across the enterprise |

## SAFe's Foundational Pillars

SAFe is built on four key bodies of knowledge:

### 1. Agile Development
SAFe builds on decades of Agile practices — Scrum, Kanban, XP — proven at the team level. Agile teams are the building blocks of all SAFe configurations.

### 2. Lean Product Development
Inspired by Lean manufacturing principles, SAFe applies Lean thinking to product and software development: optimizing flow, eliminating waste, and delivering customer value.

### 3. Systems Thinking
SAFe applies Holistic system thinking — understanding that the organization is a system of systems, and that optimizing individual parts doesn't optimize the whole.

### 4. DevOps and Continuous Delivery
SAFe integrates DevOps practices and the Continuous Delivery Pipeline to accelerate the flow of value from idea to working software in production.

## SAFe Core Values

Four core values guide behavior and decision-making across all SAFe levels:

1. **Alignment** — Shared understanding and commitment to direction
2. **Built-in Quality** — Quality is everyone's responsibility at every step
3. **Transparency** — Visible work, plans, and progress across the enterprise
4. **Program Execution** — Reliable and predictable delivery of value

## The SAFe Big Picture

SAFe is illustrated by the "Big Picture" — a visual overview of the entire framework. It shows all the roles, activities, artifacts, and flows at every level of the organization, from Portfolio to Team.

The Big Picture is organized around four levels:
- **Portfolio Level** — Strategy and investment governance
- **Large Solution Level** — For building very large systems (optional)
- **Program Level** — Agile Release Trains and Program Increments
- **Team Level** — Agile teams delivering value every Sprint

## SAFe Configurations

SAFe offers four configurations to meet the needs of different organizations:

- **Essential SAFe** — The simplest and most foundational configuration
- **Large Solution SAFe** — For large-scale systems requiring multiple ARTs
- **Portfolio SAFe** — Adds portfolio-level strategy and governance
- **Full SAFe** — The complete configuration for the largest enterprises

## Relation to Other Frameworks

SAFe is complementary to, and builds upon, many existing frameworks and methods:

- **Scrum / XP** at the team level
- **Kanban** for flow management
- **LeSS (Large-Scale Scrum)** — an alternative scaling approach
- **DORA metrics** for measuring DevOps performance
- **OKRs (Objectives and Key Results)** for portfolio alignment
