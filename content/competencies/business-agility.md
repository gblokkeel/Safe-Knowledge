# Business Agility

## Overview

**Business Agility** is the ability of an organization to compete and thrive in the digital age by quickly responding to market changes and emerging opportunities with innovative, digitally enabled business solutions.

It is SAFe's overarching goal — the reason for implementing the framework. Business Agility goes beyond software delivery agility to encompass the entire enterprise, including business units, HR, finance, legal, and all other functions.

## Why Business Agility?

The business environment has fundamentally changed:
- **Digital disruption** — Software is transforming every industry
- **Accelerating change** — Market windows open and close faster than ever
- **Customer empowerment** — Customers have more choices and higher expectations
- **Globalization** — Competition comes from anywhere in the world

Organizations that can't respond quickly are at existential risk. Being great at software delivery is necessary but not sufficient — the entire business must be able to adapt.

## The 7 Core Competencies

### 1. Lean-Agile Leadership
Leaders who embrace and model Lean-Agile mindset and behaviors. This is the **foundation** — without it, none of the other competencies can take hold.

### 2. Team and Technical Agility
High-performing Agile teams with the technical practices needed to deliver quality solutions rapidly.

### 3. Agile Product Delivery
Customer-centric, design-thinking approach to defining and building products delivered continuously.

### 4. Enterprise Solution Delivery
Applying Lean-Agile principles to build and maintain the world's largest and most complex software applications.

### 5. Lean Portfolio Management
Aligning strategy and execution, portfolio operations, and governance through Lean and Agile approaches.

### 6. Organizational Agility
Business processes and organizational structures that adapt quickly to new business opportunities.

### 7. Continuous Learning Culture
A culture of relentless learning and growth at the individual, team, and enterprise levels.

## Measuring Business Agility

Scaled Agile provides a **Business Agility Assessment** tool that helps organizations measure their current state across all 7 competencies:

### Assessment Dimensions
Each competency is scored across multiple dimensions on a 1–5 scale:
- **1 = Starting** — Initial awareness
- **2 = Developing** — Beginning implementation
- **3 = Growing** — Consistently applied
- **4 = Scaling** — Broadly effective
- **5 = Optimizing** — Continuous improvement at enterprise scale

### Using Assessment Results
- Identify the weakest competencies (your biggest growth opportunities)
- Set improvement targets
- Create a transformation roadmap
- Reassess periodically to measure progress

## Business Agility Beyond Software

True Business Agility requires transforming non-technology parts of the organization:

| Function | Business Agility Transformation |
|---------|--------------------------------|
| **HR** | Agile talent acquisition, development, and retention; team-based performance |
| **Finance** | Lean Budgets, rolling forecasts, value stream funding |
| **Legal** | Streamlined contract review; Agile procurement |
| **Marketing** | Continuous marketing; experiment-based campaigns |
| **Sales** | Agile sales processes; lean proposals |
| **Operations** | DevOps; automated operations; self-service capabilities |

## Business Agility Maturity Journey

Most organizations progress through phases:
1. **Team Agility** — Individual teams adopt Scrum/Kanban
2. **ART Agility** — Teams form ARTs; PI Planning begins
3. **Portfolio Agility** — LPM connects strategy to execution
4. **Full Business Agility** — All enterprise functions operate with agility

Most organizations that begin SAFe adoption start in phases 1–2 and work toward phases 3–4 over 2–5 years.
