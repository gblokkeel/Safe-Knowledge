# SAFe Core Values

## Overview

The **SAFe Core Values** are the four fundamental values that define the culture and the way of working within a SAFe organization. They describe the beliefs and behaviors that the enterprise must embrace to achieve Business Agility.

The four SAFe Core Values are:
1. **Alignment**
2. **Built-in Quality**
3. **Transparency**
4. **Program Execution**

These values create a powerful foundation because they reinforce each other. Transparency enables alignment; alignment enables execution; execution builds trust; trust creates the safety for more transparency.

## 1. Alignment

### What It Means
Alignment ensures that leaders and teams share a common understanding of the direction, strategy, and work priorities. Everyone in the organization — from executives to individual contributors — is working toward the same goals.

### Why It Matters
Without alignment:
- Teams work on the wrong things
- Business strategy isn't reflected in what gets built
- Teams conflict with each other over priorities
- Customers receive incoherent products

### How SAFe Achieves Alignment
- **Strategic Themes** connect the portfolio to enterprise strategy
- **Portfolio Vision** sets direction for value streams
- **PI Planning** aligns all ART teams to shared PI Objectives
- **Program Board** shows how all teams are contributing to shared goals
- **System Demo** demonstrates the aligned integrated work

### Alignment Is Two-Way
Alignment is not just top-down command. Leaders must also align **to** the needs and realities surfaced by teams. This requires:
- Regular feedback loops from teams to management
- Willingness to adjust strategy based on ground-level learning
- Transparency from both leaders and teams

## 2. Built-in Quality

### What It Means
Quality is not something that can be added at the end through testing and inspection. Quality must be built into every step of the development process — from requirements through code through deployment.

### Why It Matters
The cost of poor quality compounds over time:
- Defects found in production cost 100x more to fix than defects found at development time
- Technical debt slows down future development
- "Hardening sprints" are a sign that quality wasn't built in
- Customer-reported defects damage trust and generate rework

### SAFe's Quality Practices

**Team-Level Practices:**
- **Test-Driven Development (TDD)** — Write tests before code
- **Behavior-Driven Development (BDD)** — Acceptance tests as collaboration tools
- **Continuous Integration (CI)** — Integrate code multiple times per day
- **Refactoring** — Continuously improve code quality
- **Code Reviews** — Peer review before merge
- **Pair Programming** — Two developers working together

**ART-Level Practices:**
- **Definition of Done (DoD)** — Shared quality standards
- **System Demo** — Integrated testing at the program level
- **Automated Testing** — Tests run on every commit

**Portfolio-Level Practices:**
- **Built-in Quality** as a portfolio constraint
- **NFR tracking** — Non-functional requirements managed across the portfolio

### Built-in Quality Is an Economic Imperative
Quality isn't about perfectionism — it's economics:
- Preventing defects is cheaper than fixing them
- Clean code is faster to extend than messy code
- Built-in quality enables sustained high velocity

## 3. Transparency

### What It Means
Transparency means creating an environment where:
- Work, progress, and problems are visible to everyone
- There are no hidden agendas
- Information is shared openly, including bad news
- Trust is built through honesty

### Why It Matters
Without transparency:
- Management makes decisions based on incomplete information
- Problems fester until they become crises
- Trust erodes ("they're not telling us the real story")
- Coordination is impossible because no one knows the real state of things

### How SAFe Creates Transparency
- **Visible backlogs** — Backlogs are accessible to all stakeholders
- **Program Board** — The plan is publicly visible
- **Metrics dashboards** — Progress and health data shared openly
- **PI Objectives** — Teams' commitments are visible
- **Inspect & Adapt** — Problems are surfaced and addressed openly
- **Radiators** — Information posted where everyone can see it

### Leaders Must Model Transparency
Transparency starts at the top:
- Leaders must share real context, not sanitized messages
- Leaders must acknowledge when they don't know the answer
- Leaders must be transparent about constraints and trade-offs
- Leaders must create safety for bad news to surface

## 4. Program Execution

### What It Means
Program Execution refers to the ART's ability to actually deliver working software reliably and predictably. It is about results, not effort.

### Why It Matters
Agile values and principles are meaningless without actual delivery:
- Stakeholders won't trust teams that don't deliver
- Business opportunities are missed when delivery is unpredictable
- Technical debt explodes when features are never "done"
- Team morale suffers when work doesn't ship

### How SAFe Ensures Program Execution
- **PI Planning** — Creates a shared, coordinated plan
- **PI Objectives** — Commitments that drive accountability
- **ART Sync** — Regular cross-team coordination to prevent blockers
- **System Demo** — Objective evaluation of what was actually built
- **Inspect & Adapt** — Systematic improvement of execution capability
- **DevOps and CI/CD** — Technical infrastructure for reliable delivery

### Predictability as a Foundation for Trust
The most important benefit of good program execution is trust:
- When the ART delivers what it commits to, Business Owners trust the ART
- This trust creates the conditions for better collaboration
- Better collaboration improves both alignment and execution
- A virtuous cycle of improvement

---

## How the Values Reinforce Each Other

```
Alignment
   → Everyone knows what matters
      → Teams build the right things reliably (Execution)
         → Progress is visible and honest (Transparency)
            → Trust grows; strategy flows from business to teams (Alignment)
               → Built-in Quality ensures sustainable execution
```
