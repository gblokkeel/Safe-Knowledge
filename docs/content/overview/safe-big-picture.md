# The SAFe Big Picture

## Overview

The **SAFe Big Picture** is the visual representation of the Scaled Agile Framework. It serves as a comprehensive, one-page overview of all the roles, activities, artifacts, events, and flows within SAFe. It is organized into horizontal "levels" that correspond to different scopes of work within the enterprise.

The Big Picture is not just a diagram — it is the navigable knowledge base itself, where each element links to detailed guidance on that topic.

## Structure of the Big Picture

The Big Picture is organized into **four horizontal levels**:

### Portfolio Level (Top)
The highest level shows how enterprise strategy connects to execution:
- Strategic Themes define business direction
- Portfolio Vision translates strategy into value streams
- Lean Portfolio Management (LPM) governs investment and prioritization
- Portfolio Kanban manages the flow of Epics

### Large Solution Level (Optional)
For organizations building very large and complex systems:
- Solution Trains coordinate multiple ARTs
- Solution Intent captures system requirements and design decisions
- Pre- and Post-PI Planning events synchronize planning

### Program Level (ART)
The primary value delivery level in SAFe:
- Agile Release Trains (ARTs) are the primary delivery mechanism
- Program Increments (PIs) are the heartbeat — 8–12 week planning and delivery cycles
- PI Planning, System Demo, and Inspect & Adapt are core events

### Team Level (Bottom)
Where value is actually built:
- Agile Teams of 5–11 people
- Sprint-based or Kanban-based execution
- Built-in quality practices

## Reading the Big Picture

The Big Picture should be read **top-down** for strategy flow and **bottom-up** for value delivery:

```
Business Strategy
      ↓
Portfolio Vision & Themes
      ↓
Value Streams & ARTs
      ↓
Program Increments
      ↓
Iterations / Sprints
      ↓
Working Software
      ↑
Customer Value Delivered
```

## Key Flows Illustrated

### Value Flow
Value flows from the Portfolio Backlog → Program Backlog → Team Backlogs → Working Software → Customer.

### Demand Flow
Customer demand and market feedback flows upward through the system, informing portfolio strategy and priorities.

### Learning Cycles
At every level, inspect-and-adapt cycles capture learning and drive continuous improvement:
- Sprint Retrospectives (team level)
- Inspect & Adapt workshops (ART level)
- Portfolio retrospectives (portfolio level)

## Foundations

At the base of the Big Picture are the **Foundational Competencies** that underpin everything:

1. **Lean-Agile Leadership** — Leaders model SAFe values and principles
2. **Team and Technical Agility** — High-performing Agile teams
3. **Agile Product Delivery** — Customer-centric product development
4. **Enterprise Solution Delivery** — Building large systems with Lean-Agile
5. **Lean Portfolio Management** — Strategy and governance
6. **Organizational Agility** — Business processes that flex with change
7. **Continuous Learning Culture** — Growth mindset at every level

## How to Use the Big Picture

The Big Picture serves multiple purposes:

| Audience | How to Use |
|---------|-----------|
| **Executives** | Understand portfolio-level governance and investment |
| **Release Train Engineers** | Guide ART events and program execution |
| **Scrum Masters** | Connect team practices to the bigger picture |
| **Product Managers** | Understand how features flow from portfolio to teams |
| **Developers** | See how their work connects to business outcomes |
| **Coaches** | Use as a navigation map for SAFe education |

## SAFe Icons and Notation

The Big Picture uses consistent visual notation:
- **Swim lanes** represent organizational levels
- **Arrows** show flow of work, information, and feedback
- **Boxes and circles** represent roles, artifacts, and events
- **Color coding** distinguishes team, program, large solution, and portfolio elements

## Versioning

SAFe is continuously updated. Each major version introduces changes to the Big Picture:
- **SAFe 4.x** — Introduced DevOps and Customer Centricity
- **SAFe 5.x** — Added Business Agility and 7 Core Competencies
- **SAFe 6.x** — Emphasized flow, OKRs, and simplified guidance

Always reference the version of SAFe your organization has adopted.
