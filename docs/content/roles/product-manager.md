# Product Manager

## Overview

The **Product Manager** is the content authority for the Program Backlog. They are responsible for understanding customer needs, defining value, and ensuring the ART builds the right things. The Product Manager works at the **program level**, focusing on the overall product direction for the ART.

The Product Manager is the "what and why" owner at the ART level — distinct from the Product Owner, who owns the "what and why" at the team level.

## Responsibilities

### Own the Program Backlog
- Define and prioritize **Features** in the Program Backlog
- Apply **WSJF** to sequence features economically
- Ensure the Program Backlog reflects the Vision and Roadmap
- Groom the backlog in preparation for PI Planning

### Define Vision and Roadmap
- Maintain the ART **Vision** — the long-term direction for the solution
- Maintain the **Roadmap** — the planned delivery timeline
- Communicate vision compellingly to all ART stakeholders

### Engage with Customers
- Act as the primary voice of the customer at the program level
- Conduct market research and customer interviews
- Validate features and their acceptance criteria with customers
- Prioritize based on customer value, not stakeholder loudness

### Lead PI Planning
- Present the **Vision** at the start of PI Planning
- Walk teams through the **top 10 features** for the upcoming PI
- Review team plans and PI Objectives for business feasibility
- Score PI Objectives as Business Owner at the end of PI Planning

### Support Product Owners
- Work with Product Owners to break Features into User Stories
- Ensure Product Owners have the context needed to make team-level decisions
- Resolve conflicts between Product Owner priorities

### Manage Stakeholders
- Interface with Business Owners and executives
- Communicate program status and plans to stakeholders
- Manage expectations around scope, schedule, and capacity

## Product Manager vs. Product Owner

| Aspect | Product Owner (PO) | Product Manager (PM) |
|--------|-------------------|---------------------|
| **Level** | Team level | Program (ART) level |
| **Backlog** | Team Backlog (Stories) | Program Backlog (Features) |
| **Planning horizon** | Sprint (2 weeks) | PI (8–12 weeks) |
| **Work items** | User Stories, Defects | Features, Enablers |
| **Prioritization** | Iteration priorities | Feature priorities (WSJF) |
| **Customers** | Business representatives | External customers and market |
| **Ceremonies** | Sprint Planning, Daily Standup | PI Planning, System Demo |

## Key Artifacts Owned

- **Program Backlog** — The ordered list of features
- **Feature Definitions** — Benefits hypothesis and acceptance criteria
- **Vision** — Long-term product direction
- **Roadmap** — Planned delivery over time
- **Metrics** — Business value delivered, feature velocity

## Skills and Qualities

### Strategic Thinking
- Ability to connect market insights to product direction
- Balance short-term delivery with long-term strategy
- Understand competitive landscape

### Communication
- Present vision compellingly to diverse audiences
- Write clear Feature descriptions that teams can implement
- Interface effectively with executives, customers, and teams

### Economic Thinking
- Apply WSJF to prioritize the program backlog
- Understand Cost of Delay and its implications for sequencing
- Think in terms of business value, not feature volume

### Collaboration
- Work effectively with Product Owners (who implement the detail)
- Partner with System Architects (who define the technical approach)
- Coordinate with Business Owners (who provide strategic direction)

## Relationship with Other Roles

| Role | Relationship |
|------|-------------|
| **Business Owners** | Receive strategic direction; update on progress |
| **Product Owners** | Provide context for features; receive story-level details |
| **System Architect** | Collaborate on Enabler features; ensure technical and business alignment |
| **RTE** | Partner for ART execution and event facilitation |
| **Customers** | Primary source of value discovery |
| **Epic Owners** | Coordinate portfolio-level epics into features |

## Metrics

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Feature Velocity** | Features delivered per PI |
| **Business Value Score** | Business Owners' score of PI Objectives achieved |
| **Customer Satisfaction (NPS)** | Net Promoter Score from customers |
| **Time to Market** | Time from feature definition to deployment |
| **Backlog health** | Age of items, queue depth |

## Common Challenges

- **Feature factories** — Pressure to deliver features rather than outcomes; combat with outcome-based PI Objectives
- **Stakeholder management** — Balancing competing priorities from multiple stakeholders
- **Customer distance** — Product Managers who don't talk directly to customers make poor prioritization decisions
- **Technical understanding** — PMs need enough technical depth to understand trade-offs without micromanaging implementation
