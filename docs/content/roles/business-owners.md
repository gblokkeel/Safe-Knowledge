# Business Owners

## Overview

**Business Owners** are the key business and technology stakeholders who have the primary business and technical responsibility for governance, compliance, and return on investment (ROI) for the solution developed by an Agile Release Train (ART).

Business Owners are not a single person — they are a small group (typically 3–5) of senior stakeholders who collectively represent the business interests for the ART.

## Responsibilities

### Strategic Direction
- Provide the business context and strategic direction for the ART
- Define and communicate the business case for major features and capabilities
- Ensure the ART's work aligns with the organizational strategy

### PI Planning Participation
- Attend **PI Planning** — this is a critical responsibility
- Provide business context during the Vision and top features briefing
- Review team plans and PI Objectives during the Planning Review
- Score PI Objectives at the end of PI Planning (0–10 scale)

### Accept and Evaluate Deliverables
- Attend **System Demos** to evaluate the integrated increment
- Provide real-time feedback on what's been built
- Validate that features meet business expectations

### Govern Investment
- Monitor and govern the business value being delivered
- Make decisions about investment continuation or redirection
- Participate in Epic approval and governance

### Champion the ART
- Advocate for the ART within the organization
- Remove organizational impediments (those requiring executive authority)
- Provide political air cover for the ART to operate effectively

## Business Owners in PI Planning

Business Owners play a key role throughout PI Planning:

| PI Planning Phase | Business Owner Activity |
|------------------|------------------------|
| **Vision/Context** | Attend and understand the program's direction |
| **Feature Briefing** | Receive the top feature priorities |
| **Team Planning** | Available for questions from teams |
| **Draft PI Objective Review** | Review team objectives for business feasibility |
| **Risk Review (ROAM)** | Participate in risk identification and mitigation |
| **Final PI Objective Scoring** | Score each team's PI Objectives (0–10) |
| **PI Planning Retrospective** | Provide feedback on planning effectiveness |

## Scoring PI Objectives

At the end of PI Planning, Business Owners score each team's Committed PI Objectives on a scale of 0–10:
- **10** = Critical business value
- **7–9** = High business value
- **4–6** = Moderate value
- **1–3** = Some value but not critical
- **0** = No business value (should be removed)

This scoring creates accountability and ensures teams understand the relative business importance of their commitments.

At the end of the PI, Business Owners score **actual achievement** (again 0–10), enabling the **PI Predictability Measure**:

```
PI Predictability = Σ(Actual Business Value) / Σ(Planned Business Value) × 100%
Target: 80%+
```

## Business Owner Characteristics

Effective Business Owners:

| Characteristic | Description |
|---------------|-------------|
| **Senior enough** | Authority to make business decisions, remove organizational blockers |
| **Engaged** | Actually show up to PI Planning and System Demos |
| **Accessible** | Available to the ART throughout the PI (not just at events) |
| **Business-focused** | Represent customer and business value, not personal agendas |
| **SAFe-informed** | Understand their role in the Agile process |

## Business Owners vs. Sponsors vs. Stakeholders

| Role | Relationship to ART |
|------|---------------------|
| **Business Owners** | Active participants in ART governance; score PI Objectives |
| **Sponsors** | Fund the work; may or may not attend ART events |
| **Stakeholders** | Interested in outcomes; consulted but not governance participants |

## Common Business Owner Anti-Patterns

| Anti-Pattern | Impact |
|-------------|--------|
| **Absent Business Owners** | Teams plan without business context; features miss the mark |
| **Proxy Business Owners** | Low-level attendees without authority can't make decisions |
| **Feature-directing BOs** | BOs prescribe features without understanding technical constraints |
| **Score inflation** | All objectives scored 10; no meaningful prioritization signal |
| **No scoring follow-through** | BOs score at PI Planning but not at I&A; no accountability loop |

## Enabling Business Owners to be Effective

Organizations should:
- Educate Business Owners on their role in SAFe
- Ensure Business Owners have time blocked for PI Planning and System Demos
- Give Business Owners real authority to make decisions
- Invite Business Owners to customer conversations
