# Lean Portfolio Management (LPM)

## Overview

**Lean Portfolio Management (LPM)** is the function that aligns strategy and execution in SAFe. It applies Lean and Systems Thinking approaches to strategy and investment funding, Agile portfolio operations, and governance.

LPM is one of SAFe's 7 Core Competencies and represents the highest level of the SAFe portfolio. It answers the question: "How do we ensure that our portfolio of investments is aligned to strategy and delivering value efficiently?"

## The LPM Function

LPM is not a single person or role — it's a **function** performed by a cross-functional group that includes:
- **Enterprise Executives** (CEO, CTO, CFO, etc.)
- **Business Managers** (Portfolio Managers, Product Line managers)
- **Enterprise Architects**
- **Lean-Agile Center of Excellence (LACE)** members

Together, this group exercises the three dimensions of LPM.

## Three Dimensions of LPM

### 1. Strategy and Investment Funding

**Connecting the portfolio to enterprise strategy:**
- Maintain the **Portfolio Vision** — the long-term aspirational view
- Define and communicate **Strategic Themes** — business objectives that connect portfolio to enterprise strategy
- Identify and organize **Value Streams** — how value flows to customers
- Maintain the **Portfolio Canvas** — a one-page summary of the portfolio

**Lean Budgeting:**
Instead of project-based funding (allocating money to projects), LPM uses **Lean Budgets** allocated to Value Streams:
- Each Value Stream/ART gets a budget guardrail
- Teams have autonomy within the guardrail
- Budgets are reviewed and adjusted periodically (not locked for the year)
- Participatory Budgeting involves the teams in allocation decisions

**Portfolio Kanban:**
- Manages the flow of Epics from ideation to implementation
- Ensures proper analysis and evaluation before committing to large investments
- Provides visibility into the portfolio's largest work items

### 2. Agile Portfolio Operations

**Coordinating and supporting ARTs:**
- Establish Agile portfolio operations policies and practices
- Support the LACE in driving Agile adoption
- Coordinate across Value Streams and ARTs
- Manage cross-portfolio dependencies

**Operational Excellence:**
- Track and improve flow metrics across the portfolio
- Ensure ARTs have the organizational support they need
- Address systemic impediments that ARTs can't resolve

### 3. Lean Governance

**Compliance, risk, and expenditure:**
- Ensure the portfolio meets regulatory and compliance requirements
- Manage portfolio-level risks
- Govern spending within approved guardrails
- Track and assess value delivered vs. investment made

**Participatory Budgeting:**
A collaborative budget allocation process:
1. Each value stream presents their needs
2. Teams and managers participate in the allocation decision
3. Budget allocations are made with team input
4. Allocations are reassessed each PI or at major milestones

## Key Artifacts

| Artifact | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Portfolio Vision** | Long-term aspirational view of the portfolio |
| **Portfolio Canvas** | One-page portfolio overview |
| **Strategic Themes** | Business objectives from the enterprise strategy |
| **Portfolio Backlog** | Ordered list of Epics and Enablers |
| **Portfolio Kanban** | Visual flow management for Epics |
| **Portfolio Roadmap** | Planned delivery of capabilities over time |
| **Value Stream Budget** | Budget allocation by value stream |

## Lean Budgets vs. Traditional Project Funding

| Traditional | Lean Budgets |
|------------|-------------|
| Fund projects | Fund value streams |
| Annual budget cycles | Quarterly or continuous adjustment |
| Detailed upfront business cases | Lightweight Epic hypothesis |
| Budget approved for specific deliverables | Budget guardrails with team autonomy |
| Kill decisions are rare and painful | Pivot/stop decisions are routine |
| Variance tracking against plan | Flow and value metrics |

## Portfolio Kanban States

The Portfolio Kanban moves Epics through stages:

```
Funnel → Review → Analyzing → Portfolio Backlog → Implementing → Done
```

| State | Activity |
|-------|---------|
| **Funnel** | Raw ideas captured |
| **Review** | Quick assessment of alignment and viability |
| **Analyzing** | Lightweight business case (Lean Business Case) |
| **Portfolio Backlog** | Approved; waiting for capacity |
| **Implementing** | ARTs actively working on the Epic |
| **Done** | Epic delivered; value assessed |

## Epic Governance

Major investments are managed as **Epics**:
- **Business Epics** — New capabilities with significant business impact
- **Enabler Epics** — Infrastructure and architectural work

Each Epic has a **Lean Business Case**:
- Hypothesis statement
- Leading indicators of success
- Non-functional requirements (NFRs)
- In/out of scope
- Analysis summary
- Go/No-Go recommendation

## LPM Cadence

| Event | Frequency | Purpose |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| **Portfolio Sync** | Weekly/Bi-weekly | Operational status of value streams |
| **Strategic Portfolio Review** | Quarterly | Strategy alignment and investment review |
| **Participatory Budgeting** | 1–2x per year | Collaborative budget allocation |
| **Portfolio PI Planning** | Optional | Align portfolio-level plans |
